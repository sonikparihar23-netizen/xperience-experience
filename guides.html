How buying a ready home works

  1. 01 Week 1 Set budget & finance Agree your budget and get mortgage pre-approval if you need a loan.
  2. 02 Weeks 1–3 View & choose Shortlist with your Xpert and view in person or by video call.
  3. 03 Day 0 Agree & sign MOU Sign Form F (MOU) and pay a 10% security deposit, held by the broker.
  4. 04 +1–2 weeks Seller NOC The developer confirms no outstanding service charges and issues the NOC.
  5. 05 +1 day Transfer at DLD Pay the balance and fees at a Trustee office; the title deed is issued.
  6. 06 Same week Keys & set-up Collect keys, connect DEWA and register for chiller and internet.

Jargon buster

Dubai property terms

DLD Dubai Land Department — the government body that registers every property transaction.
RERA Real Estate Regulatory Agency — licenses brokers and regulates rents, escrow and contracts.
Form F (MOU) The standard memorandum of understanding signed by buyer and seller once a price is agreed.
NOC No Objection Certificate from the developer, needed before a resale can transfer.
Oqood The DLD interim register for off-plan purchases, before a title deed exists.
SPA Sale & Purchase Agreement — the contract between buyer and developer on off-plan sales.
Ejari Mandatory online registration of every tenancy contract in Dubai.
Freehold Areas where foreign nationals can own property outright, including the land.
