Renting FAQs

How long does it take to rent a home in Dubai?+

Usually one to two weeks. Having your documents ready and starting your search early helps speed things up.

What is Ejari?+

Ejari is the Dubai Land Department's system for registering tenancy contracts. Registration makes your lease legally recognised and is needed to connect utilities.

How do I set up water and electricity?+

Tenants register with DEWA using their Ejari certificate, tenancy contract and ID. DEWA then activates the service and bills you monthly.

Should I choose a short-term or long-term rental?+

Short-term rentals are usually furnished and need no residency visa. Long-term annual leases cost less per month but require a residency visa and Emirates ID.

Can my landlord raise the rent or ask me to leave?+

Rent increases follow the RERA rental index and need 90 days' notice before renewal. Eviction for reasons such as sale or personal use needs 12 months' notice.
