Overview

About Ellington Properties

Ellington Properties is a boutique property development firm in Dubai that further extends its services to investors and landowners for asset management and consultation. With over 75 years of regional and international property development and real estate experience, Ellington was founded in 2014 to offer unique living solutions within the competitive landscape that bring a new level of sophistication to the city of Dubai. Ellington endeavours to craft beautiful environments for exceptionally high-quality lifestyles. Inspired by art and reflective of their owners’ aspirations, Ellington residences are classic in feel but contemporary of vision, canvases to be filled with the experience and exuberance of life. Ellington’s current projects include high-rise luxury residences and multi-family communities in Dubai, located in the prestigious Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Palm Jumeirah, Jumeirah Village Circle and Majan area of Dubai Land. Where contemporary meets classic, Ellington Properties envisions a new way of living for discerning homeowners in Dubai and beyond.

Questions

Ellington FAQs

What is Ellington Properties Known for?+

Ellington Properties is known for developing design-driven, boutique residential communities in Dubai with a strong focus on architecture, interior detailing and high-quality craftsmanship. The developer is recognised for its minimalist, contemporary design philosophy and premium lifestyle amenities.

Does Ellington Properties offer flexible payment plans?+

Yes, Ellington typically provides investor-friendly payment structure such as 60/40, 70/30 and post-handover plans depending on the project. These plans support both long-term investors and end-user buyers seeking manageable instruments.

What are the most popular projects by Ellington properties?+

Top Projects include Ellington Beach House, Ocean House, The Quayside, DT1 Downtown, The Sloane, The Ellington House series and Arbor view. These developments stand out for modern interiors, resort-style amenities and elevated architecture.

What makes Ellington Properties different from other Dubai developers?+

Ellington is known for its design-first philosophy, boutique living concepts and meticulous attention to detail. Unlike mass developer, Ellington focuses on premium low-destiny communities, high-quality finishes and curated amenities that appeal to luxury buyers.
