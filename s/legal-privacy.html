Privacy policy Terms of use

Xperience Realty Real Estate L.L.C is committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who uses our website. This policy explains what information we collect from visitors, how we use it and the rights you have over it.

1. What we offer

Xperience Realty is a real estate agency for buying, selling, renting and investing in Dubai property. Our services include:

  • Property management
  • Property listings
  • Real estate sales
  • Property advertising arrangements
  • Identifying qualified buyers
  • Property valuation
  • Competitive market analysis
  • Marketing and advertising services
  • Negotiation
  • Tenancy contract management

2. What data we collect

Xperience Realty collects the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Curriculum vitae (CV)
  • Details of your participation in recruitment events, including date and location

3. How we collect your data

When you register for services on any website affiliated with Xperience Realty, we may ask for personal information including your name, email address, postal address, phone number and date of birth. Different pages may ask for different information.

By entering your details in the fields provided, you allow Xperience Realty and its service providers to deliver the services you have selected.

We also use cookies and collect IP addresses. We use analytics software to review IP addresses and cookies so we can improve your experience. This information is not used to build a personal profile of you, and log files are cleared regularly.

4. How we use your data

We collect your data to:

We keep your data confidential except where the law requires disclosure, for example to government agencies or law enforcement. We may use third parties to process your data, under strict confidentiality agreements.

  • Process your requests and manage your account
  • Send you emails with special offers on products and services
  • Improve the services you have subscribed to
  • Invite you to take part in surveys about our services
  • Market to you, only with your explicit consent

5. Your data protection rights

You have the following rights:

To exercise any of these rights, contact us on +971 56 408 0888 or +971 4 704 9400, or email info@xrealty.ae.

  • Right to access: ask for copies of your personal data (a small fee may apply)
  • Right to rectification: ask us to correct information you believe is inaccurate
  • Right to deletion: ask us to delete your personal data, subject to certain conditions
  • Right to restrict processing: ask us to limit how we process your data, subject to certain conditions
  • Right to object: object to our processing of your personal data, subject to certain conditions
  • Right to data portability: ask us to transfer your data to another organisation or directly to you

6. How we use cookies

We use cookies, including cookies placed and read by third-party providers, to improve your experience. Cookies help us to:

Most mobile devices accept cookies automatically, but you can turn them off in your device settings. Some features of the website may not work properly if you do.

  • Identify account holders
  • Track new users
  • Record session information
  • Keep you signed in
  • Understand how the website is used, for advertising
  • Improve how the website works

7. Types of cookies we use

We use two types of cookies:

  • Functionality cookies: recognise returning visitors and remember your preferences, such as language and location
  • Advertising cookies: collect information about your visit, such as the content you viewed, the links you followed, your browser and your IP address, for advertising purposes

8. Privacy policies of other websites

Our website contains links to other websites. This policy applies only to our website, so if you follow a link to another site, please read its privacy policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We review this privacy policy regularly and publish any updates on this page.
