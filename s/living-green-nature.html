The lifestyle

Dubai's green communities put parks, landscaping and sustainability at the centre. Expect walking and cycling paths, energy-efficient homes and plenty of space to spend time outdoors.

They suit families who want a healthier, quieter lifestyle and buyers who care about their environmental footprint. Many also offer schools, shops and clinics within the community.

Choose from solar-powered villas, townhouses or energy-efficient apartments. We can help you find the community that matches your lifestyle and budget.

Good to know

What makes a community green?+

Green communities focus on landscaped open space, energy-efficient building and infrastructure that lowers everyday energy use.

Are green communities good for families?+

Yes. Most have parks, play areas and schools nearby, which makes them popular with families.

What homes are available?+

You can choose from villas, townhouses and apartments, depending on the community.
