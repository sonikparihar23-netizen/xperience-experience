Dubai Hills Estate FAQs

What is it like to live in Dubai Hills Estate?+

A 2,700-acre master community by Emaar and Meraas, built around an 18-hole championship golf course and Dubai Hills Mall. Highlights: Championship golf, Dubai Hills Mall, Dubai Hills Park, Leading schools.

How far is Dubai Hills Estate from Downtown Dubai, DXB Airport and Dubai Marina?+

Typical drive times are about 15 minutes to Downtown Dubai, 25 minutes to DXB Airport and 18 minutes to Dubai Marina, depending on traffic.

Who developed Dubai Hills Estate?+

Dubai Hills Estate is developed by Emaar Properties and Meraas.
